English r: the broad symbol /r/ usually represents [ɹ], unlike French /ʁ/. The sequences /tj, dj, nj/ can occur in tune, dew, new ; some accents drop the /j/.

Also useful: /hw/ whine in some accents; /x/ loch ; /ʔ/ uh-oh . Weak endings: /i/ happy , /u/ fruition . /əl, ən, əm/ may be [l̩, n̩, m̩] in bottle, button, rhythm .

Accent differences are normal: cot/caught or marry/merry/Mary may sound alike. This is a practical key, not an inventory of every English accent.
