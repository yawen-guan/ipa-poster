# English

vowels

short:      /ɪ/,  /ʊ/,  /ʌ/,  /ɒ/,  /ə/,  /ɛ/(1), /æ/
long :      /iː/, /u:/, /ɑː/, /ɔː/, /ɜː/
diphthongs: /iə/, /uə/, /aɪ/, /ɔɪ/, /eə/, /eɪ/,
                        /aʊ/, /oʊ/(2)

1: /ɛ/ is transcribed with ⟨e⟩ in many dictionaries. However, /eɪ/ is also sometimes transcribed with ⟨e⟩, especially in North American literature, so ⟨ɛ⟩ is chosen here.
2: /oʊ/ is often transcribed with ⟨əʊ⟩, particularly in British literature, based on its modern realization in Received Pronunciation. It is also transcribed with ⟨o⟩, particularly in North American literature.

consonants:
voiceless:  p, t, tʃ, k, f, θ, s, ʃ
voiced:     b, d, dʒ, g, v, ð, z, ʒ
            m, n, ŋ,  h, w, r, l, j, dj
